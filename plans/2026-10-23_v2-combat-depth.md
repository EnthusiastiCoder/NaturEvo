Typed attacks (3 hero attacks, per-species monster movesets) apply status effects (knockback, stagger, stun, confused, slowed, bleed, poison); attacker recovery, victim hit-recovery and block recovery; nets sense attack type/phase and statuses and choose attack type; block is no longer free; duels start close enough that an enemy is visible most of the time; retrain, re-evaluate on held-out panels over 3 seeds; viewer shows all of it; campaign and docs updated. ## Standards and constraints
User request (verbatim): "attacks based on their type should have status effects on the hit character. like knockback or stagger or stun etc. and there should be recovery times". Also: "I see those mfs blocking even from far away" and "why did the characters block even when no enemy was in sight at all?"
User answers (verbatim): statuses = Knockback, Stagger, Stun, Slow/bleed, "have a confused effect, slowed effect, bleed effect and poison effect." Attack types = "3 hero attacks, monsters by species": light (fast, stagger), heavy (slow windup, knockback or stun) and a lunge or sweep for the hero; each monster species gets a different moveset (elites and boss differ too); nets learn to pick the right attack and read the opponent's windup. Recovery = "Both: attacker and victim": every attack has windup, active and recovery frames, attacker vulnerable in recovery (whiffs punished); victim has hit-recovery; blocking also has recovery (guard drops then a short delay).
