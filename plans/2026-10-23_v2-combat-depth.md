## Goal

### Combat contract (all constants in `config.rs`, struct `CombatTuning` field `ArenaConfig.combat`, `Default` = values below; ticks at 30 Hz)
States (CombatState, as_str exactly): Idle, Windup, Active, Recovery, Blocking, Dodging, Dead (existing) plus GuardRaise, GuardRecover, Staggered, Stunned, Knocked. 12 states, `state_idx` 0..11 in that order.
Action gains `attack_kind: u8` (0 light, 1 heavy, 2 special). Latched when a swing starts; slot not owned by the fighter maps to `kind % n_moves`.
Attack def `Move {name, windup, active, recovery, damage, range, half_arc, stamina_cost, lunge (tiles moved forward during active), effects: [Effect]}`, `Effect {status, chance 1.0 only (deterministic), duration, magnitude}`. Data tables in attacks.rs, species `Hero, Minion, Rogue, Warrior, Mage` (idx 0..4) and rank `Grass, Elite, Boss` (monster only). Table:
- Hero Light: wu4 ac3 rc6 dmg8 rng1.2 arc90 cost5; stagger 8. Hero Heavy: wu12 ac4 rc14 dmg18 rng1.3 arc90 cost15; knockback 2.0 tiles; stun 20 only if victim was Blocking (guard break) or Recovery. Hero Lunge: wu8 ac3 rc12 dmg10 rng1.6 arc60 cost12 lunge 2.0 tiles; slowed 90, stagger 6.
- Minion: Swipe (light-like, stagger 8) + Bite (bleed 150). Rogue: Stab (wu3, fast, bleed 150) + Dash (lunge, slowed 90). Warrior: Club (wu14 heavy, knockback 2.5, stun 24) + Cleave (arc 140, stagger 10). Mage: Hex (rng 2.0, wu10, poison 240, confused 90, low dmg 5) + Zap (stagger 8, slowed 60). Elite: same moves, damage x1.15 and one extra effect per move (distinct from grass: minion adds slowed 60; rogue adds poison 120; warrior adds bleed 90; mage adds knockback 1.0). Boss: 3 moves = species base two + Slam (wu16 ac4 rc16 dmg20 stun 30 knockback 2.0) and damage x1.2. Totals must keep each move's windup+active+recovery within 13..46 ticks. Every status must appear in at least one move.
Status rules (`status.rs`; per fighter array indexed Knock, Stagger, Stun, Confused, Slowed, Bleed, Poison; each holds remaining ticks, stacks, source Role):
- Stagger: interrupts Windup (counter-hit) but NOT Active (committed swing); fighter enters Staggered for `duration` ticks, cannot act, may only turn at 0.5; refresh = max(existing,new), no stacking; after it ends 12 ticks stagger immunity (poise). Dodging i-frames and Dead ignore all effects.
- Stun: Stunned state, no action/move/turn, guard dropped; stun immunity 45 ticks after it ends; diminishing returns: duration x 0.5^n where n = stuns received in the previous 300 ticks, floor 6 ticks. Stun cannot be chained (test: 20 consecutive heavy hits yield at most 1 stun per 45+ ticks). Hit-stun also cancels Blocking.
- Knockback: displacement `distance` tiles over 6 ticks along attacker-to-victim line, state Knocked (no control). Stops at wall/obstacle (circle_blocked); if it stops short by more than 0.3 tiles the victim takes `wall_slam_damage` 3 and Stagger 10 once. Blocking halves the distance; Dodging ignores; knock immunity 10 ticks after it ends. Moving fighter cannot be pushed into the other fighter (uses can_stand).
- Confused: 90 ticks default; refresh = max, no stacking. Voluntary fwd/strafe vector is rotated by +-60 deg (sign flips every 15 ticks, from integer hash of (seed,tick,index)) and turn sign inverted on alternate 15-tick windows; attack/block/dodge bits unaffected.
- Slowed: stacks to 2; speed factor 0.6^stacks (floor 0.36) and turn x0.7; each application refreshes duration to max.
- Bleed: stacks to 3; each stack deals 1 hp every 15 ticks (first tick 15 after application); duration refresh to max; damage can kill; bleed ticks DOUBLE while the fighter's |fwd| > 0.5 (moving makes it worse).
- Poison: no stacks; duration extends additively (cap 300); 1 hp every 15 ticks; can NOT reduce hp below 1; halves stamina regen while active.
- DoT damage counts in damage_dealt for the source role. Statuses with positive magnitude are not applied if the hit was fully blocked (bleed, poison, confused, slowed skipped; blocked stun degrades to stagger 8; blocked knockback halved).
Recovery rules: attacker Recovery duration x `whiff_recovery_mult` 1.5 if the swing hit nothing; fighter in Recovery, Staggered, Stunned, GuardRecover takes damage x `punish_mult` 1.25 and the hit is logged `punish:true`. Hit-recovery = the Stagger/Stun above. Block: `Blocking` requires GuardRaise first (6 ticks, no protection, no attack/dodge); on release or stamina 0 -> GuardRecover (8 ticks, 20 on guard break; no attack/dodge/block, move x0.5); hold drains `block_hold_drain` 6 stamina/s; absorbed hit costs 15; stamina does not regen while GuardRaise/Blocking; guard break (stamina hits 0 on an absorbed hit) -> Stunned 20. Parry: hit landing in the first 5 ticks after GuardRaise ends blocks 100 percent and staggers the attacker 14, logs `parry`. BLOCK_HALF_ARC = PI/4 (90 degrees, strictly narrower than vision half-FOV 1.047), front only, nothing behind. Attacks need stamina >= move cost (deducted at start). Dodge unchanged except cannot start from Staggered/Stunned/Knocked/Recovery/GuardRecover.
Log contract (FightLog JSON, also read by viewer): per frame add `"atk":[a0,a1]` (0..2, -1 none), `"fx":[[7 remaining ticks],[7]]` in order knock,stagger,stun,confused,slowed,bleed,poison, `"seen":[b0,b1]` (did that fighter see the enemy when deciding), `"ev":[...]` events: `{"t":"hit","by":0|1,"atk":k,"dmg":x,"blocked":b,"punish":b,"fx":[status idx...]}`, `{"t":"dot","to":i,"kind":"bleed"|"poison","dmg":x}`, `{"t":"parry","by":i}`, `{"t":"slam","to":i}`, `{"t":"guard_break","to":i}`. Fight-level `"species"`, `"rank"`. New fields optional for older viewers (parse missing as none).
Sensor contract (`sense.rs`, N_IN = 80, all in [-1,1], enemy block zero unless seen): 0 hp, 1 stamina, 2 dodge cooldown, 3 own phase progress (timer/phase total), 4..=15 own state one-hot (12), 16..=22 own status remaining/max, 23..=25 own attack kind one-hot, 26..=30 own species one-hot (hero=0), 31 enemy dist, 32/33 sin/cos bearing, 34 enemy hp, 35 enemy stamina, 36..=47 enemy state one-hot, 48 enemy phase progress, 49/50 enemy facing sin/cos, 51..=57 enemy status remaining/max, 58..=60 enemy attack kind one-hot, 61..=65 enemy species one-hot, 66 seen, 67 in attack range (or in its move range), 68 bias (always 1), 69/70 own position, 71..=79 wall rays. Export `pub const` index names for each block in `sense::idx` (e.g. OWN_STATE, OWN_FX, ENEMY_STATE, ENEMY_FX, ENEMY_ATK, SEEN). Net: N_OUT = 9: 0-2 analog, 3 attack, 4 block, 5 dodge, 6/7/8 attack-kind logits (argmax, ties pick lowest), N_HID = 24 (fallback 16 only if the training budget below is missed; N_PARAMS follows).
Duel start: spawns move to (17.5, h/2+0.5) and (30.5, h/2+0.5), 13 tiles apart, inside the guaranteed-open centre band (x 14..34, y 13..19), both facing each other. Justification: vision range 12 means the enemy is seen after about 0.1 s of approach so visible share >= 85 percent of ticks (was 25-50 percent at 41 tiles); keeps a short approach with real positioning; the hero's map walk, zones, elites, boss and arena seeds in the jungle campaign are unchanged (only duel start distance changes), and obstacle placement keeps the old near_spawn clearing plus the centre band. Rejected: a scripted approach phase (adds a second mode for nets and viewer).
Fitness (evolve.rs `score_match`, weights as named consts in config `FitnessTuning`): remove `+1.5*min(fb,0.12)`; keep `+0.5*min(fa,0.3)`; add `-2.0 * share_of_ticks(guard states and enemy unseen or dist>4)`; `DRAW_PENALTY` 6.0 (draw strictly worse than a no-damage loss); passivity `-1.0 * share_of_ticks beyond 300 since last damage event, cap 1.0`; `+0.6` per punished hit (cap 3 per match); `+0.15` per distinct status applied on the opponent (cap 6 per match); stamina-floor term not added. `GenStats` gains metrics from metrics.rs; checkpoint and CSV carry a version tag and old checkpoints are rejected with a clear error.
Metrics (`metrics.rs`, pure function of a FightLog): action shares (attack, block, dodge, move, idle, involuntary) bucketed by seen/unseen x distance (close<=1.2, mid<=4, far<=12, vfar); statuses applied per fight by type; punish hits; draw flag; attack-kind shares; `decisive` = non-draw. CLI `arena-sim eval --run DIR --gens 49,99,149,199 --seeds 32 --out report.json` prints means by role over the held-out panel (opponents from other-seed runs).
Campaign library screen: `evolve::screen(genome, role, panel, seeds) -> Screen {block_unseen_share, block_share, draw_rate, statuses_per_fight}`; `Library::from_dir` drops champions with block_unseen_share > 0.10 or block_share > 0.25 or draw_rate > 0.25 (screen on a fixed 8-fight panel, deterministic) and then applies `keep`; if everything fails it errors with the numbers. Monster species and rank come from encounter kind and zone (Grass -> Minion/Rogue/Warrior/Mage by seeded pick, Elite -> same species Elite rank, Boss -> Warrior or Mage Boss) and are recorded per encounter; training assigns species by `seed % 4` and rank Grass for most matches (every 8th Elite, every 32nd Boss).
