# v3 plan: seeded sword-and-shield move vocabulary, learned grace, rule-driven lethality

## Goal
User request (verbatim): "also can we seed in some cool moves from other games? like our ai doesnt use the sword gracefully and usefully. it has to learn the grace and also be lethal and punishing."

Seed a vocabulary of 13 sword/shield moves plus Idle (14 net choices). The net outputs a move choice plus 4 continuous parameters (reach, speed, arc, delay) and a drive, instead of 12 raw joint targets. A deterministic executor turns (move, params) into smooth keyframe joint targets. The AI must LEARN when, how and with what grace to use each move. Lethality and punishment come from GAME RULES (posture break, whiff recovery, commitment, punish windows, guard break), never from fitness bonuses. Grace is emergent (continuous params, timing, footwork spacing, an energy cost on jerk) and is only MEASURED (metrics), not rewarded.

Research (read first): research/2026-10-24_sword-move-vocabulary.md (sources: Sekiro, Elden Ring wiki parry windows 3-7 frames/0.10-0.23 s, For Honor guard break 300-400 ms, Mordhau feint/drag/riposte, HEMA Liechtenauer cuts). Its frame data is thin; all phase timings below are our design numbers, tunable in config, and flagged as such. Gaps (Chivalry, Toribash damage formula, HEMA half-sword/pommel) are UNVERIFIED and not relied upon.

## Design

### Vocabulary: 13 moves + Idle (research-backed pick, 14 net choices; Drag and Chain are parameter/rule, not moves)
MoveId order is fixed (it is the net output index): 0 Idle, 1 Overhead, 2 DiagDown, 3 DiagUp, 4 Sweep, 5 Thrust, 6 LowCut, 7 Feint, 8 Parry, 9 Guard, 10 Riposte, 11 ShieldBash, 12 Kick, 13 Backstep. N_MOVES = 14.
Times in seconds at speed param 0.5 (W windup / A active / R recovery); all in config.rs / moveset.rs table (design proposals, not sourced). Required joints (proximal to distal) drive move_possible; any listed joint's part disabled/destroyed => impossible; partial damage => lower torque_scale => lower tip speed and reach by the existing PD physics (no fake multiplier).

Params (net outputs tanh mapped to 0..1): reach (scales shoulder/elbow extension and forward lunge step), speed (scales phase durations 0.7x..1.4x; faster = more tip speed = more impact but more energy and wider timing risk), arc (angle/tilt of the cut, height of target zone), delay (extra windup hold 0..0.2 s = Drag; mistimes parries; costs energy 3). Footwork is the continuous `drive` (spacing is the net's job).

### Executor (src/executor.rs, src/moveset.rs): keyframes, not raw joints
Each move = windup pose, active pose(s), recovery pose, normalised 12-joint targets in moveset.rs (`pose_at(id, params, phase, p) -> [f32;12]`). Interpolation is minimum-jerk quintic (10p^3-15p^4+6p^5) between keyframes, so grace is guaranteed smooth at the target level and the physics PD does the rest. State machine Idle->Windup->Active->Recovery->Idle. Commitment rule: once Active starts nothing cancels it; Windup can be cancelled only by Feint (before 60% of windup) or by Backstep when threatened (costs energy 5); last 25% of Recovery allows a chain into another attack (chain window). If the net asks for a move while committed it is ignored (no penalty). A move cannot start when Fallen/Staggered/KO, when `move_possible` false, or when energy < the move's cost (move costs energy by speed and mass, beyond the existing motor cost; add jerk energy: energy += JERK_COST * |d2 target / dt2| integrated, i.e. smooth moves are cheaper: this is physics, not reward). Contact during Active marks `contact_made`; Active ending with none => `whiffed` and the Recovery is flagged Open (see rules). The executor outputs `Action{targets, drive}` exactly as scripted.rs does, so duel.rs integration is one branch in `decide`.

### Rules that make skill lethal and punishing (src/rules.rs; constants in config.rs; NO fitness changes)
1. Whiff recovery = Open: a fighter in Recovery after a whiff (no contact) takes damage x1.30 and posture gain x1.5 (Sekiro/FH punish logic). Hit or blocked moves have no Open flag (only normal recovery, still uncancellable).
2. Counter-hit: damage taken during an opponent-visible Windup or Active phase of one's own move: x1.15 (committing carelessly costs).
3. Stagger exposed multiplier stays 1.5; multipliers combine as max(), capped 1.75, never stack unbounded.
4. Parry success: attacker staggered (existing perfect-parry rule: +40 posture) and defender gains riposte window 0.6 s; Riposte inside the window starts with a 0.15 s windup and deals x1.5; outside the window its windup is 0.30 (ordinary cut). Parry miss/mistimed: Parry Recovery 0.30 with sword locked, defender Open x1.2 (For Honor 2017 early-parry rule).
5. Guard-break: ShieldBash into a guarding/blocking opponent: posture +35, guard disabled 0.4 s, then Open x1.25 for the same 0.4 s (a free light follows).
6. Heavy blocked: Overhead/Sweep add x1.4 posture on a block (guard is not free; shield integrity also drops).
7. Backstep counter on Thrust: attacker posture +30 and Open for 0.3 s.
8. Feint costs 8 energy, Drag 3, chain +5; energy at 0 caps torque (existing) so spam is physically self-defeating.
Damage still flows through the existing damage_part(zone, armour_eff, per-hit cap, pool death) pipeline; rules only supply multipliers and posture additions. Numbers are tunable constants; any retune is reported, never hidden.

### Net IO and sensing (net.rs, sense.rs, duel.rs)
Outputs N_OUT = 19: 14 move logits (argmax with ties to lowest index, a start only if its logit exceeds Idle's by MOVE_MARGIN 0.05 and the move is allowed), 4 params, 1 drive. 12 raw joint outputs are removed. Hysteresis: a decision happens every DECISION_TICKS (as it does now); between decisions the executor runs at 60 Hz. Added inputs (N_IN grows from 104 to ~140, tests reference N_IN symbolically): own move id one-hot (14), own phase one-hot (4) + phase progress, committed flag, Open flag, riposte window remaining, guard-broken remaining, energy cost of last move; opponent move id one-hot (14), phase one-hot (4) + progress, Open flag, recovery remaining. Opponent move/phase is visible telegraph information (a human sees the windup), not hidden state, so it is fair and learnable; opponent inputs still zero when own head is disabled. N_PARAMS stays within 4000..7000 (hidden 32, ctx 8): update core_determinism_tests ranges (N_OUT==19, N_IN in 90..=160). Old champions/runs (slice_s1..s3) are incompatible with the new genome size: keep them untouched as history, new runs go to v3/runs/vocab_s1..s3.

### Scripted opponent that uses the vocabulary (src/skillbot.rs; scripted.rs untouched)
SkillBot(seed, tier) picks moves through the same Executor so telegraphs and rules are identical. Tier 1 "Brawler": picks distance-appropriate attacks at random intervals (like old Scripted but with all attack moves). Tier 2 "Reactive": additionally Parry/Guard when a live threat is detected (reaction delay 0.2 s, deterministic jitter), punishes the net's Open recovery with the fastest in-range attack. Tier 3 "Duellist": adds Feint and Drag baits, Backstep counter versus Thrust, ShieldBash against turtling, Riposte after a successful parry. Deterministic from seed. The old Scripted and Dummy stay available.

### Grace metrics (src/grace.rs; reported in eval, never in fitness)
Per fighter per match: jerk = mean squared third derivative of sword-tip position over Active+Recovery windows (lower is smoother; report normalised by tip speed); spacing consistency = std of (distance at attack start minus move reach) over attacks (lower is better); hits_per_swing = body hits / attack starts; whiff_rate = whiffed / attack starts; punish_rate = hits landed on an opponent that was Open or Staggered / opponent Open-or-Staggered windows entered; recovery_efficiency = nominal recovery / actual time to next guard-ready; move_usage_entropy and per-move share; parry_success_rate; feint_bait_rate (feints followed by an opponent whiffed parry); damage_per_energy. Sanity floors tested on scripted fights: metrics finite, in range, deterministic.
