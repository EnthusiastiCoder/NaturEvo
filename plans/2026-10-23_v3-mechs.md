# v3 plan: big-robot duels with per-part combat (NaturEvo)

## Goal
Build v3/ (new branch `feat/v3-mechs`, cut from `feat/v2-arena`; v1/ and v2/ untouched and green) : a zero-dependency Rust sim where two giant humanoid robots, each with a physical sword (right) and shield (left), fight in a side-view arena. Neural controllers are evolved. Fighters must visibly attack AND defend: dodge, shield, deflect with the sword. Each move depends on named body parts; damaged or destroyed parts degrade or remove moves. Hits have a location, direction and impact that drive damage, knockback, hit-stop, sparks. Viewer: PixiJS + Vite, vector-drawn crisp robots, record mode, landing page with run picker and aggregate results.
Phase 1 (vertical slice, ships first): one robot design, 1v1 arena duel, defence working and visible, training, viewer, landing. Phase 2: arena cut from the map tile, map + campaign integration.

Technical: zero third-party Rust crates (std only; std::thread::scope allowed); deterministic (same seed => byte-identical log; fixed dt, seeded splitmix RNG, no HashMap iteration in sim, threaded results collected in index order); viewer PixiJS ^8 + Vite + TS + vitest (copy v2/viewer toolchain). UI rules carried over: low cognitive load, no page scroll, tooltips on all controls, Lucide icons, light/dark theme, one accent colour; the landing at the base URL must never show a demo banner. v1/ and v2/ test suites must still pass at the end (run `cargo test` in v2/sim and `npm test` in v2/viewer once, before the final verifier).
Lessons from research/2026-10-23_learned-behaviour.md that v3 MUST NOT repeat: (a) holding a guard must cost energy every tick; (b) no context-free fitness bonus for a guard/block bit (there is no block bit at all in v3: defence is physical pose + collision); (c) draw must be strictly worse than any loss; (d) start close (5 m apart in a 14 m arena, not 41 tiles apart) and sensors see the whole arena (no FOV gaps); (e) penalise guarding with no live threat; (f) anti-stalemate bleed; (g) measure means over 3 seeds, not one run.

## Design (the spec implementers follow)
Units: metres, kg, seconds. Physics dt = 1/60 s. Net decides every 3 ticks (20 Hz), joint targets held between. Match max 900 ticks (15 s). Side view: x horizontal, y up, ground at y=0; each robot has a facing sign (+1/-1) flipped to face the opponent when grounded and not mid-swing (turning costs 0.25 s, i.e. cannot spin instantly). Robot height 4.0 m standing (arena 14 m wide, 6 m high view).

### 1. Robot body (src/robot.rs)
13 rigid capsule parts, 11 revolute joints, kinematic chain (FK, no rigid-body solver; PD motors move joint angles):
| Part | Mass kg | Parent joint | HP | Armour | Zone mult | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head | 40 | Neck (+-35 deg) | 60 | 0.30 | 1.6 | sensors; head HP<50% adds noise to opponent inputs, 0 disabled = blind (opponent inputs zeroed), destroyed = KO |
| Torso | 300 | root (Waist joint twist/lean +-30 deg torso-vs-pelvis) | 220 | 0.45 | 1.0 | core: power transfer, houses energy; destroyed = KO |
| Pelvis | 180 | root | 140 | 0.40 | 1.0 | hips, balance; disabled = cannot stand (fall) |
| UpperArm L/R | 70 | Shoulder (-30..+170 deg) | 80 | 0.30 | 0.8 | reach, power |
| Forearm L/R | 50 | Elbow (0..140 deg) | 70 | 0.30 | 1.2 (exposed joint) | carries sword (R) or shield (L) |
| Thigh L/R | 120 | Hip (-60..+90 deg) | 110 | 0.35 | 0.9 | locomotion, kick power |
| Shin+Foot L/R | 90 | Knee (0..130 deg) plus Ankle (-30..+30 deg) as separate joint with Foot as 13th/14th small capsule | 90 | 0.30 | 1.2 (knee) / 0.6 (foot) | balance, kick |
Implement as 13 listed capsules: Head, Torso, Pelvis, UArmL, UArmR, FArmL, FArmR, ThighL, ThighR, ShinL, ShinR, FootL, FootR (Shin+Foot split; use the table row values for each, foot HP 60 mass 40 zone 0.6). 11 joints: Neck, Waist, ShoulderL/R, ElbowL/R, HipL/R, KneeL/R, AnkleL/R = 12 (count is 12; outputs below = 12 joints + 1 drive). Sword: capsule 2.2 m, mass 25, attached to FArmR hand, never damaged (indestructible but dropped if FArmR destroyed). Shield: plate 1.2 m on FArmL, shield integrity 100, absorbs; at 0 it is dropped (disabled).
Motor: PD toward net target angle; max angular velocity per joint and max torque scaled by part health: `torque_scale = clamp(hp_frac / 0.5, 0.25, 1.0)` for hp_frac>0; part disabled (hp 0): joint limp (target = relaxed pose, speed x0.1); destroyed (hp <= -50% of max, accumulating overkill): part and all distal children detach (fall as debris), their mass leaves the CoM; sword/shield dropped if forearm destroyed.
Tankiness depends on health: `armour_eff = armour * (0.4 + 0.6*hp_frac)` (cracked armour protects less). Damage to part = `impact * zone_mult * (1 - armour_eff)`; capped impact per hit (`IMPACT_CAP`).
Energy (single pool, 100): regen 8/s when no motor above 50% speed and no guard; motor cost ~ sum |omega|*k; shield guard (shield plate raised in front, i.e. arm pose above threshold) drains 5/s; swing costs by joint speed. At energy 0 all torque_scale capped at 0.3 (sluggish) and shield deactivates. No repair in fight.
Balance and falling: CoM = mass-weighted part centres (surviving parts only). Support = x-span of grounded feet (lowest foot grounded; foot within 0.08 m of ground). Margin = distance of CoM x inside span (negative outside). Impacts add impulse to a CoM-velocity term; margin < 0 for > 0.4 s, or destroyed/disabled leg parts (a disabled thigh/shin = margin penalty -0.5 m each; destroyed leg = automatic fall) => Fallen state for 1.5 s, get-up requires both hips+one knee not disabled; cannot attack while fallen, takes x1.5 damage. Root height solved each tick so the lowest foot touches ground. Root x integrates `drive` output (-1..1, max 2.0 m/s scaled by leg torque_scale, costs energy), cannot pass arena bounds or solid obstacles (obstacles are axis-aligned boxes standing on the ground; robot capsules blocked horizontally; jumping out of scope).
Stagger/posture meter (0..100, decays 15/s after 0.5 s without hit): hits add `impact*0.05` (clash adds to BOTH fighters, defender gets less when parrying well); >=100 => Staggered 0.8 s (cannot act, targets forced to a recoil pose) and a x1.5 damage multiplier "exposed" window (Armored Core strain / Sekiro posture), meter resets to 40.

### 2. Moves, quality, weapon, defence (src/moves.rs, src/combat.rs)
Controller outputs joint targets directly; moves are NOT selected. Combat recognises them post hoc at contact and for fitness/telemetry/log labels. Move table (required parts; if a required part is disabled the move cannot be performed, enforced physically because that joint is limp, and the move label is reported "impossible" in telemetry):
| Move | Chain (proximal -> distal) | Also needs | Delivered by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slash (R) | Pelvis/HipR, Waist, ShoulderR, ElbowR | FArmR+sword, stance foot grounded | sword swept capsule |
| Thrust (R) | Hip R, Waist, ShoulderR, ElbowR (extension) | sword | sword tip |
| Shield bash (L) | Waist, ShoulderL, ElbowL | shield intact | shield plate |
| Kick (R or L) | Pelvis, Hip, Knee, Ankle | opposite leg grounded and balance margin>0 | foot |
| Guard | ShoulderL, ElbowL (shield raised between opponent and torso/head) | shield, energy>0 | shield plate collision |
| Parry/deflect | ShoulderR, ElbowR, Waist (sword blade crosses incoming blade) | sword, forearm R | sword vs sword swept collision |
| Dodge | Hips, Knees, Ankles, `drive` (step back, duck = hip+knee flex lowers head ~1.2 m, lean = Waist) | both legs not disabled, balance | no collision: swept blade misses |
Strike quality at the contact tick: `q = A * Tm * H * B * (impact scale)`:
- Tip velocity along strike: `v = sum_j (omega_j x r_j)` using FK lever arms from each chain joint to the contact point; contribution `c_j = ((omega_j x r_j) . n) / |v|` clamped >= 0. Effective mass `m_eff = sum_j c_j * (mass of all links distal to joint j)` + weapon mass. Alignment `A = max(0, v_hat . n_hit)` where n_hit is the contact normal into the target.
- Timing `Tm = 0.6 + 0.4 * frac` where `frac` = fraction of consecutive proximal->distal pairs whose peak |omega| times (in the preceding 0.5 s) are in order (kinetic chain). Perfect chain = 1.0, reversed = 0.6.
- Health factor `H = mean over chain joints of torque_scale_j` (a damaged elbow reduces every slash, a destroyed ShoulderR makes sword moves impossible).
- Balance factor `B = clamp(0.5 + margin/0.5, 0.5, 1.0)` for kicks; 1.0 for others except `B` x0.8 if stagger meter > 70.
- Raw impact `I = 0.5 * m_eff * (v . n)^2 * (0.5+0.5*A) * Tm * H * B`, capped at IMPACT_CAP, then zone damage as in section 1. Examples that tests lock: perfect-order chain Tm=1 vs reversed Tm=0.6 gives >=1.5x impact; ElbowR hp_frac 0.25 reduces slash impact vs full by factor torque_scale (0.5); destroyed ShoulderR => slash and thrust impossible; kick with a disabled opposite leg gets margin penalty and falls.
Collision: each tick sweeps (start-of-tick to end-of-tick transform, 2 sub-samples) sword capsule, shield plate and foot against the opponent's capsules, sword, shield; segment-segment closest point with radii; O(parts^2) per tick. Resolution order per swing per tick: (1) sword-vs-sword => clash: if defender blade angle crosses the attacker's within 35 deg of perpendicular and defender blade speed toward the attacker blade > 1 m/s => Deflect (attacker sword angular velocity reversed x0.4, attacker posture +25 x quality, defender posture +5; "perfect parry" if the defender's blade started moving within the last 0.25 s => attacker stagger +40 and defender gains 0.3 s of free energy regen), else Clash (both blades lose speed 50%, both posture +15); (2) sword vs shield plate (shield intact, energy>0) => Block: impact reduced by 85%, shield integrity -= 0.3*impact, attacker posture +10, defender posture +10 x (1-integrity_frac*0.5), the remaining reduced impact is applied to the shield arm part (FArmL); shield at 0 integrity drops; (3) sword vs body capsule => Hit at contact point p, direction n = normalised relative velocity at p.
Hit reaction: knockback impulse on defender root x = `k * I * n.x / total_mass` (and hip rotation kick on the struck part's joint angle as a transient offset decaying in 0.2 s along n), hit-stop: both fighters freeze 3 to 6 ticks scaled by I (a render and sim effect: sim time-dilates both equally, logged), CoM impulse added for balance, struck part posture, event logged with point, direction, impact, zone, damage, part hp after. Sparks/clash logged as events.
Hit-stop is applied as skipped sim ticks of motor integration (both fighters' joint velocities frozen) so determinism holds.

### 3. Sensing, actions, network, fitness (src/sense.rs, src/net.rs, src/fitness.rs)
Decision (research recommendation, confirmed): direct continuous joint control, 12 joint target angles (normalised -1..1) + 1 `drive` = 13 outputs. No move menu and no block bit.
Inputs (~100, normalised, all in own facing frame, mirrored so "forward" = +x; opponent always visible, no FOV; head damage adds noise/zeroes opponent inputs): own 12 joint angles, 12 joint angular velocities, 13 part hp fractions, energy, posture, balance margin, grounded/fallen flags, own sword tip pos+vel (4), shield centre pos (2), 13 part contact flags (touched in last 0.1 s), last impact magnitude + direction (3); opponent key points relative to own torso (head, torso, pelvis, sword tip, sword hilt, shield centre, front foot, rear foot = 16), opponent sword tip velocity (2), opponent sword speed toward me (1) and its time-to-contact estimate along the current path (1) and threat zone height (1) (i.e. "attack phase"), opponent 13 part hp fractions (zero if own head disabled), opponent posture and stagger flag, distance (1), distances to the arena walls/obstacles left and right (2), line-of-sight flag (obstacle between torso centres; obstacles are low and blades go over, flag informs only) (1).
Network: recurrent MLP reusing v2 net design (fast_tanh): input N_IN -> hidden 32 tanh (+8 recurrent context units) -> 13 outputs. ~5k weights. Genome = flat f32, mutation sigma annealed, uniform crossover (copy v2 genome.rs + net.rs and change sizes).
Fitness per match (src/fitness.rs; all terms normalised, weights in config.rs and unit-tested):
 `score = 4*win + 3*dmg_dealt_norm (zone weighted: head x1.5, core x1.0, limbs x0.7, sums normalised by opponent total hp) - 2.5*dmg_taken_norm + 2.0*defence_credit - 1.0*idle_guard - 1.0*passivity + 0.5*chain_quality + KO bonus`; draw = -6 (strictly worse than any loss, which is -4 at worst without defence credit and about -4..-1 otherwise).
 defence_credit: a "live threat" is detected by the sim when an opponent blade or foot has speed > 2.5 m/s and its predicted swept path comes within 0.4 m of a defender body capsule within 0.4 s. Each threat is resolved exactly once as: Hit (no credit, damage counted), Parried/Deflected (credit 1.0 x min(1, I_potential/IMPACT_REF)), Blocked (0.7 x), Dodged (the swept path missed by >0.1 m and the defender moved >= 0.3 m or flexed a joint >= 20 deg during the threat, 0.7 x), Miss-without-reaction (0). Credit capped at 6 threats per match and halved when the same defender took a hit from that swing's follow-up inside 0.5 s. This is the defence incentive and cannot be farmed by guarding.
 idle_guard: ticks with shield raised (plate between opponent and head/torso) or sword in guard pose and no live threat in the last 0.6 s, divided by total ticks (energy drain also bites).
 passivity: seconds with distance > 3.5 m and no hit exchanged in last 4 s, divided by max seconds; plus energy-starvation is not credited. Anti-stalemate bleed: after tick 450 both fighters lose 0.5 torso HP/s (shrinking time) so draws are rare.
 chain_quality: mean q of the fighter's landed hits (rewards coordinated motion over flailing); flailing (high joint speed, no contact) pays energy anyway.
Curriculum (by generation, per fighter): gen 0-30 stage A: 70% vs static dummy robot standing (learn stand, balance, reach, hit; defence terms off), 30% vs scripted attacker; gen 30-80 stage B: 50% vs scripted attacker (scripted swings: slash high/low, thrust, kick at random intervals 0.8-2.0 s; forces defence signal to exist; deterministic from seed) + 50% self-play; gen 80+ stage C: self-play against the current population plus Hall of Fame (champions saved every 10 gens, 8 kept, pick uniformly) plus 1 scripted attacker per evaluation. Opponents per individual per gen: 3 (stage A 2). Roles: hero and monster are two co-evolving populations like v2 (same code path, different arena role); robots identical design, colour differs.
Evolution loop: copy v2 evolve.rs structure (tournament/elitism, population 64, 200 gens, checkpoint, training.csv, champions saved), adapted to the new fitness; plus periodic held-out evaluation `eval` command over 3 training seeds reporting mean and range of: win rate vs HoF, defence_rate = (parried+blocked+dodged)/live threats, hit_rate, idle_guard_share, draw share, mean match length, per-defence-type share.
Performance budget: 64 pop x 3 opponents x 900 ticks = 173k ticks/gen; target <= 10 us per tick (two robots FK, ~120 capsule-pair tests, two net forwards every 3rd tick) => about 1.7 s/gen single-threaded, 200 gens under 6 min single-thread, under 2 min with std::thread::scope across cores (results gathered by index, so deterministic). Test: a micro-benchmark test asserting a 900-tick match under 25 ms in release (loose; not run in debug) and a train smoke of 3 gens.

### 4. Arena and map (Phase 2: src/arena.rs, src/campaign.rs)
Phase 1 arena: flat ground 14 m wide, 2 to 3 low obstacle boxes generated from the seed (0.6 to 1.5 m high), walls at both ends, biome params (ground colour/material, backdrop id) from a `Biome` enum.
Phase 2: the map tile of the encounter (v2 world is a boolean tile grid, v2/sim/src/world.rs, plus the campaign map in campaign.rs) is "cut" into the arena: the tile's blocked cells along a horizontal slice through the encounter point (taken at the row where the fighters spawn) become obstacle boxes (heights from consecutive blocked cells, min 0.6 m, max 2 m) at proportional x positions; the tile's biome gives ground material, backdrop and palette; arena width fixed 14 m, deterministic from (map seed, tile coords). Camera transition: the map viewer zooms (about 0.6 s) from the top-down tile onto the encounter, crossfades to the side-view cut of the same tile (ground strip derived from the tile's colours), then fights. Hero robot (the same part-list robot, larger scale marker) walks the map with the v2 hero-walking code; encounters and permadeath logic copied from v2 campaign with robots instead of v2 fighters. Biome recommendation: change the setting from jungle to a ruined "scrapland / abandoned industrial" biome (rusted ground, debris obstacles, concrete walls) because giant robots in a jungle read oddly and metallic palettes make sparks pop; biome is a data enum so jungle stays selectable (open question 2).

### 6. Log and run formats (v3/sim/src/log.rs; contract between sim and viewer)
Fight JSON `{ "version":3, "arena":{width,height,obstacles:[{x,w,h}],biome}, "parts":[names], "dt":0.0333, "frames":[ { "t":n, "f":[ {fx, facing, parts:[[x1,y1,x2,y2] x 13 in cm integers], sword:[x1,y1,x2,y2], shield:[x1,y1,x2,y2], hp:[13 ints 0..100 percent], energy, posture, state:"idle|staggered|fallen|ko"}, {..} ] } ], "events":[ {t,kind:"hit",victim,part,x,y,dx,dy,impact,zone,dmg} | {kind:"clash",x,y,dx,dy,type:"parry|clash|perfect_parry"} | {kind:"block",x,y,impact} | {kind:"dodge",who} | {kind:"stagger",who} | {kind:"fall",who} | {kind:"destroyed",who,part} | {kind:"hitstop",ticks} | {kind:"ko",who} ], "result":{outcome,ticks,defence:{parry,block,dodge,hit}x2} }` logged at 30 Hz (every 2 ticks); viewer draws capsule endpoints directly (no FK in TS, so no mismatch). Run folder `v3/runs/<name>/`: training.csv, champions `champion_<role>_gen_<n>.json`, `fight_gen_<n>.json`, `run.json` (config, seed, per-gen eval summary), `eval_seeds.json` (3-seed aggregate).
