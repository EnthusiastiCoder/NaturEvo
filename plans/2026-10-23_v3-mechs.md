# v3 plan: big-robot duels with per-part combat (NaturEvo)

## Goal
Build v3/ (new branch `feat/v3-mechs`, cut from `feat/v2-arena`; v1/ and v2/ untouched and green) : a zero-dependency Rust sim where two giant humanoid robots, each with a physical sword (right) and shield (left), fight in a side-view arena. Neural controllers are evolved. Fighters must visibly attack AND defend: dodge, shield, deflect with the sword. Each move depends on named body parts; damaged or destroyed parts degrade or remove moves. Hits have a location, direction and impact that drive damage, knockback, hit-stop, sparks. Viewer: PixiJS + Vite, vector-drawn crisp robots, record mode, landing page with run picker and aggregate results.
Phase 1 (vertical slice, ships first): one robot design, 1v1 arena duel, defence working and visible, training, viewer, landing. Phase 2: arena cut from the map tile, map + campaign integration.

## Design (the spec implementers follow)
Units: metres, kg, seconds. Physics dt = 1/60 s. Net decides every 3 ticks (20 Hz), joint targets held between. Match max 900 ticks (15 s). Side view: x horizontal, y up, ground at y=0; each robot has a facing sign (+1/-1) flipped to face the opponent when grounded and not mid-swing (turning costs 0.25 s, i.e. cannot spin instantly). Robot height 4.0 m standing (arena 14 m wide, 6 m high view).

### 1. Robot body (src/robot.rs)
13 rigid capsule parts, 11 revolute joints, kinematic chain (FK, no rigid-body solver; PD motors move joint angles):
| Part | Mass kg | Parent joint | HP | Armour | Zone mult | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head | 40 | Neck (+-35 deg) | 60 | 0.30 | 1.6 | sensors; head HP<50% adds noise to opponent inputs, 0 disabled = blind (opponent inputs zeroed), destroyed = KO |
| Torso | 300 | root (Waist joint twist/lean +-30 deg torso-vs-pelvis) | 220 | 0.45 | 1.0 | core: power transfer, houses energy; destroyed = KO |
| Pelvis | 180 | root | 140 | 0.40 | 1.0 | hips, balance; disabled = cannot stand (fall) |
| UpperArm L/R | 70 | Shoulder (-30..+170 deg) | 80 | 0.30 | 0.8 | reach, power |
| Forearm L/R | 50 | Elbow (0..140 deg) | 70 | 0.30 | 1.2 (exposed joint) | carries sword (R) or shield (L) |
| Thigh L/R | 120 | Hip (-60..+90 deg) | 110 | 0.35 | 0.9 | locomotion, kick power |
| Shin+Foot L/R | 90 | Knee (0..130 deg) plus Ankle (-30..+30 deg) as separate joint with Foot as 13th/14th small capsule | 90 | 0.30 | 1.2 (knee) / 0.6 (foot) | balance, kick |
Implement as 13 listed capsules: Head, Torso, Pelvis, UArmL, UArmR, FArmL, FArmR, ThighL, ThighR, ShinL, ShinR, FootL, FootR (Shin+Foot split; use the table row values for each, foot HP 60 mass 40 zone 0.6). 11 joints: Neck, Waist, ShoulderL/R, ElbowL/R, HipL/R, KneeL/R, AnkleL/R = 12 (count is 12; outputs below = 12 joints + 1 drive). Sword: capsule 2.2 m, mass 25, attached to FArmR hand, never damaged (indestructible but dropped if FArmR destroyed). Shield: plate 1.2 m on FArmL, shield integrity 100, absorbs; at 0 it is dropped (disabled).
Motor: PD toward net target angle; max angular velocity per joint and max torque scaled by part health: `torque_scale = clamp(hp_frac / 0.5, 0.25, 1.0)` for hp_frac>0; part disabled (hp 0): joint limp (target = relaxed pose, speed x0.1); destroyed (hp <= -50% of max, accumulating overkill): part and all distal children detach (fall as debris), their mass leaves the CoM; sword/shield dropped if forearm destroyed.
Tankiness depends on health: `armour_eff = armour * (0.4 + 0.6*hp_frac)` (cracked armour protects less). Damage to part = `impact * zone_mult * (1 - armour_eff)`; capped impact per hit (`IMPACT_CAP`).
Energy (single pool, 100): regen 8/s when no motor above 50% speed and no guard; motor cost ~ sum |omega|*k; shield guard (shield plate raised in front, i.e. arm pose above threshold) drains 5/s; swing costs by joint speed. At energy 0 all torque_scale capped at 0.3 (sluggish) and shield deactivates. No repair in fight.
Balance and falling: CoM = mass-weighted part centres (surviving parts only). Support = x-span of grounded feet (lowest foot grounded; foot within 0.08 m of ground). Margin = distance of CoM x inside span (negative outside). Impacts add impulse to a CoM-velocity term; margin < 0 for > 0.4 s, or destroyed/disabled leg parts (a disabled thigh/shin = margin penalty -0.5 m each; destroyed leg = automatic fall) => Fallen state for 1.5 s, get-up requires both hips+one knee not disabled; cannot attack while fallen, takes x1.5 damage. Root height solved each tick so the lowest foot touches ground. Root x integrates `drive` output (-1..1, max 2.0 m/s scaled by leg torque_scale, costs energy), cannot pass arena bounds or solid obstacles (obstacles are axis-aligned boxes standing on the ground; robot capsules blocked horizontally; jumping out of scope).
Stagger/posture meter (0..100, decays 15/s after 0.5 s without hit): hits add `impact*0.05` (clash adds to BOTH fighters, defender gets less when parrying well); >=100 => Staggered 0.8 s (cannot act, targets forced to a recoil pose) and a x1.5 damage multiplier "exposed" window (Armored Core strain / Sekiro posture), meter resets to 40.
