### WS2 v2-sim-core (sonnet): arena physics, combat, determinism
Location v2/sim (Rust crate `arena-sim`, no deps). Files: Cargo.toml, src/{lib.rs, rng.rs (xoshiro/PCG seeded), config.rs (all constants, validated), world.rs (arena grid + obstacles from a map seed), fighter.rs, physics.rs, combat.rs, match.rs, log.rs (fight record as compact JSON frames), main.rs (CLI: `fight`, `train`, `replay`)}.
Design: fixed timestep (30 ticks/s); arena continuous coordinates (f32 pair, but arithmetic order fixed; use integer fixed-point if cross-platform float determinism matters, see Q8); circular fighters; velocity with acceleration, friction and turn-rate limits; facing angle; circle-vs-tile collision with sliding, fighter-fighter push. Actions per tick (net outputs): forward/back, strafe, turn, attack, block, dodge (dash with i-frames). Mechanics: attack has windup, active, recovery, range, arc, damage; block reduces damage by a factor in a facing arc and drains stamina (guard-break at 0); dodge costs stamina, short invulnerability, cooldown; stamina regenerates when idle; HP; match ends at death or tick cap (draw). Class stats: hero (balanced, melee) and monster (heavier, slower, different reach) kept asymmetric but near-balanced so neither side wins forever; configurable roles via config only.
Tests (v2/sim/tests): physics_tests (no tunnelling, walls stop movement, speed caps, turn-rate cap), combat_tests (hit only in arc and range, windup then active then recovery order, block reduces damage in arc not from behind, guard break, dodge i-frames and cooldown, stamina never negative, death ends match, tick cap draws), determinism_tests (same seed gives identical logs over 100 matches; different seed differs; log round-trips).
Acceptance: all green; 1000 matches of random policies finish under 2 s in release; no panics under random actions (fuzz test).

### WS3 v2-sensors (sonnet)
Files: v2/sim/src/sense.rs, tests/sense_tests.rs.
Design: vision cone (default 120 degrees, range 12 units) with 9 rays across the cone; each ray returns normalised distance and a hit class one-hot (wall, enemy, nothing); occlusion by obstacles (enemy behind a wall is not seen); plus proprioception inputs: own hp, stamina, attack/dodge cooldowns, own velocity in body frame, enemy relative bearing and distance only if in cone (else zeros plus a short-term "last seen" memory decayed over time), enemy attack-state (windup visible: the cue that makes blocking learnable), recurrent memory slot (net hidden state, see WS4). Input vector fixed size N_IN (about 40) documented in config.
Tests: ray hits wall at correct distance; enemy outside cone invisible; occluded enemy invisible; enemy windup flag visible only in cone; input vector length constant; normalised in [-1,1]; deterministic.
Acceptance: green; sensing cost is under 20 percent of tick time.

### WS4 v2-neuro (sonnet): net and genome
Files: v2/sim/src/net.rs, genome.rs, tests/net_tests.rs.
Design: feed-forward MLP N_IN to 16 hidden (tanh) to 12 hidden (tanh) to N_OUT (about 7), with a small recurrent context (previous hidden layer 1 fed back, Elman) for short-term memory; flat `Vec<f32>` genome (about 1k weights); output decoding: continuous axes in [-1,1], buttons by threshold. Genome serialise to/from JSON text with a header (version, shape, role, generation, fitness). No dependencies.
Tests: forward pass shape and known fixed weights give known outputs; NaN-free for extreme inputs; serialise round trip identical; identical genome gives identical actions; recurrent state resets per match.

### WS5 v2-evolution (sonnet; opus only if training fails to produce non-trivial behaviour after tuning)
Files: v2/sim/src/evolve.rs, fitness.rs, hof.rs, train.rs, tests/evolution_tests.rs, tests/train_smoke_tests.rs.
Design: two populations (hero pop 64, monster pop 64) co-evolving. Each generation every individual fights K opponents sampled from: current opposing population (sampled) and a hall of fame of past best opposing champions (every generation's champion kept, sampled uniformly and weighted to recent) to prevent cycling; randomised arena maps and spawn positions per match from the generation seed. Fitness (clear, shaped, anti-degenerate): win bonus, damage dealt minus damage taken, survival time fraction, small shaping for facing/closing early (annealed to zero), penalties for stalling (no damage dealt for N seconds) and wall-hugging; draws score low for both. Selection: elitism (top 10 percent copied), tournament selection, uniform crossover optional, Gaussian mutation with decaying sigma plus occasional larger mutation; seeded. Training CLI `train --gens 200 --seed S --out v2/runs/S` writes per generation: stats (best/mean fitness per role, win rate vs HoF, avg fight length, mean damage, action histogram (attack/block/dodge frequency, a measure of "learning to defend")), champion genomes (champion_gen_N.json), HoF index; checkpoint/resume; deterministic given seed. Evaluation matches parallelised only with std::thread with deterministic reduction order (results independent of thread count).
Tests: same seed gives identical champions twice; elitism never decreases best fitness of same-opponent re-evaluation; mutation changes genomes but keeps shape; HoF grows by 1 per generation; checkpoint-resume equals uninterrupted run; smoke: 30 generations at small pop run in under 60 s and trained hero beats random-weights monster in at least 80 percent of 50 matches and beats generation-0 champion (learning proof); behaviour test: trained fighters block or dodge more often when an enemy windup is visible than when not (learned defence), measured over replay logs; no collapse into permanent stalling (mean fight damage above a floor).
Acceptance: smoke tests pass; a full 200-generation run completes within the Q9 time budget and the dashboard shows rising win rate vs HoF.

### WS6 v2-map-visuals (sonnet; art decisions blocked on Q2, Q4)
Files: v2/viewer/{map.js or map.ts, tiles.js, palette.css tokens}, v2/sim/src/world.rs (richer generator), tests.
Design: a larger, more detailed arena generator: biomes (jungle floor, dense grass, mud, shallow water, ruins, stone), seeded value/fractal noise plus domain warp (ported from InfiniClash terrain.ts approach; same hashed value-noise and smoothstep idea, rewritten in the v2 stack, attributed), obstacle sets (trees with trunk/canopy layers, boulders, ruin walls, pillars, water that blocks, mud that slows), cover clusters generated to give the nets something to learn (pillars to hide behind), always symmetric-fair spawn points and guaranteed connectivity. Rendering: layered tile drawing with per-tile variation, edge blending between biomes, soft shadows, ambient particles (leaves, fireflies, dust), depth sorting by y, light vignette; character sprites from the CC0 KayKit bakes (Skeleton atlases for monsters; Barbarian/Knight/Rogue/Mage bakes for heroes if Q2 chosen) at 8 directions mapped to the fighter's facing; animation states idle/walk/attack/block/dodge/hit/death mapped to the 95 baked animations. Facing, vision cone and rays drawn as a toggleable overlay. The sim holds only collision geometry; visuals are a skin (as in InfiniClash D007), so training stays fast.
Tests: generator determinism by seed; connectivity (all spawns mutually reachable); obstacle share within 8-25 percent; spawn fairness (equal path distance within 10 percent); viewer pixel checks like v1 (obstacles distinct from floor, contrast at least 3:1, both themes); asset licence check: every file under v2/viewer/assets listed in v2/assets/CREDITS.md with licence CC0.
Acceptance: map looks clearly richer than v1 (side-by-side screenshot reviewed by the user), 60 fps replay at 1280x720 on this machine.
